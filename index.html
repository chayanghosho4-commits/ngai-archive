How the archive is organised

  1. Scope: new reactions, catalytic methods and total synthesis from JACS, Angew. Chem., ACS Catal., Nature Synthesis, Nature Chemistry, Nature Communications, Nature, Science and Chem. Inorganic, materials, polymer, energy and biochemistry papers are left out.
  2. A collector runs every 2 days and keeps the 120 most clearly synthetic papers of each month.
  3. Use the panel on the right to pick a view, a month and a journal. The page address then links straight to that selection, so you can share it.
  4. Click a TOC graphic to see it enlarged; click the title or DOI to open the paper.
  5. TOC graphics: Nature journals and Chem appear automatically. For JACS, ACS Catal., Angewandte and Science, the collector searches each paper's title in image search and keeps the picture only when it comes from a page about that same paper; the optional TOC picture helper can fill any gaps.
