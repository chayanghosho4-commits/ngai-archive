Reaction of the Week

TOC graphics and links for the latest synthetic organic chemistry papers, collected automatically every two days.

How the archive is organised

  1. Scope: we keep new reactions, catalytic methods and total synthesis from JACS, Angew, ACS Catal., Nature Synthesis, Nature Chemistry, Nature Communications (organic chemistry), Nature, Science and Chem. Inorganic, materials, polymer and biochemistry papers are left out.
  2. A collector runs every 2 days and adds new synthetic organic papers from the tracked journals, keeping the 100 most clearly synthetic papers of each month.
  3. Click a journal name to see only that journal's papers, within the month or tab you are viewing. The page address then links straight to that view, so you can share it.
  4. Papers from the current week appear under This week as the weekly reaction roundup.
  5. Once a week is older than 4 weeks, it is folded into the Monthly archive.
