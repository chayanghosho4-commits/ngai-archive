TOC Picture Helper

ACS (JACS, ACS Catal.), Wiley (Angewandte) and Science refuse automated servers, so the nightly collector cannot fetch their TOC graphics. Your own browser can. This bookmark visits each paper's page on the publisher's site, takes its TOC graphic (for Science: the summary figure, or Figure 1) and saves them all in one file for you to upload.

Checking which papers still need a picture…

1Add the bookmark (once)

Drag this button onto your browser's bookmarks bar. If you can't see the bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: ⌘+Shift+B).

📥 Get TOC pictures

New version (v5, 25 Oct 2026), for ACS's new website and for Science. ACS moved to a new platform (Silverchair) on 24 July 2026, which broke the old bookmark. If you added the bookmark before, delete the old one (right-click it → Delete) and drag this button again. The panel's title shows v5 when the new one runs. It starts fresh, so papers the old versions could not find are tried again.

Dragging doesn't work? then add a new bookmark by hand, name it Get TOC pictures, and paste the code as its address (URL).

2Open a publisher site and click the bookmark

Open one of these in this browser, wait until the page has loaded, then click 📥 Get TOC pictures on your bookmarks bar:

pubs.acs.org: JACS ASAPpubs.acs.org: ACS Catal. ASAPonlinelibrary.wiley.com (Angewandte)science.org (Science)

A small panel appears at the top right and shows the pictures as it finds them, about 2–5 seconds per paper and up to 100 papers per click. On ACS the bookmark first takes the graphical abstracts shown on the page you are on, then reads the ASAP list and every 2026 issue of JACS and ACS Catal. (these list pages show the graphical abstracts), and only visits single papers for what is still missing. ACS keeps its pictures on a separate image server that a bookmark is usually not allowed to copy from; in that case it saves the picture links, and the archive downloads the pictures itself right after you upload the file. Papers that are only “Just Accepted” have no pictures yet; they are retried on later days. When it finishes, a file named like toc-acs-2026-10-25-….json is saved to your Downloads. If more papers are waiting, click the bookmark again for the next 100.

3Upload the file

Open the upload page (data/inbox) ↗

  1. Drag the toc-….json file(s) from your Downloads onto the upload page.
  2. Click Commit changes.

That starts the archive update automatically, and the pictures appear on the site within a few minutes. After the first time, doing this once a week is enough.

?If pictures are not found

At the end the panel lists why papers were not found. Take a screenshot of the panel and send it. If it says the site is refusing the bookmark, open any one article on that site, let it load fully, then click the bookmark from that article page.

?If the panel says it could not read the list

Some sites block reading the list from this page. Click here, then paste it into the box in the panel and press Start.
